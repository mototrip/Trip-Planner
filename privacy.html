Privacy Policy

Effective / last updated: 21 November 2026 · Owner & developer: Satish Kumar Gupta

This Privacy Policy describes the current MotoTrip – Ride Planner web/PWA release at mototrip.github.io/Trip-Planner. MotoTrip is operated by Satish Kumar Gupta, who is responsible for publishing this version. This policy does not automatically cover future Android/iOS wrappers, cloud accounts or third-party SDKs if their data practices change.

1. Information entered in the app

You may enter trip names and routes (start, stops and destination), selected map coordinates, travel dates and distances, bike or car profile details, budgets, food/hotel/fuel/toll estimates, riding gear checklists, rider names, group expenses, payment/settlement records and theme preferences. Do not enter passwords, financial account credentials or other sensitive details.

2. Local data storage and access

This web version primarily saves app entries in your browser's localStorage on your device. It currently has no user account, app-owned cloud database or automatic synchronization. Entered trip and expense records are not automatically uploaded to MotoTrip's owner. Other users of the same device/browser profile may be able to access your records. The app does not encrypt your saved trip data itself.

3. Map and hosting services

  • GitHub Pages: serves this website. Your browser connects to GitHub to load its files, which can involve IP addresses and technical request logs subject to GitHub's policies.
  • OpenStreetMap tile servers: loading the Route map requests map image tiles from an OpenStreetMap-operated service. Network metadata such as IP address is shared with that service.
  • Photon (Komoot): when you type at least three letters into a place-search field, your query is sent to Photon to display suggested locations, including district/state/country when available. When you explicitly tap My location and permit access, coordinates may be sent to Photon for reverse place-name lookup. Your IP address may be visible to this third-party map provider. The demo service has availability and rate limitations.
  • Google Maps: opening a directions link sends the entered route start, destination and limited waypoints to Google Maps. It opens separately and Google's policies then apply.

The app requests device GPS location only when you tap a My location button and grant browser permission. Coordinates from GPS or chosen search results may be stored in browser localStorage on your device and sent to Google Maps if you open driving directions. You can clear a location or delete all local data; your phone controls GPS permission. Exact GPS position can be sensitive: avoid sharing public backups containing route data.

4. Ads, analytics and payments

The current website has no app-owned analytics or advertising SDK, account registration, payment processor or money transfer system. Expense settlements represent user-entered bookkeeping records, not executed bank transactions. Advertising, cloud features or added Android/iOS SDKs would require review and a policy update.

5. Your choices, retention and deletion

Use My Trips → Export Backup to save a JSON backup. That file is unencrypted and is handled by your device and wherever you choose to store it. Use My Trips → Delete all local data to remove this app's saved trip/rider/expense/profile data in the current browser; you can also clear site storage in browser settings. Local records otherwise remain until deleted or cleared by the browser. Deletion on one device does not remove separately exported backups, Google Maps history or other third-party service logs.

6. Data security

The website is served over HTTPS. The current app limits scripts to its own origin via a browser Content Security Policy meta tag, but this is not a guarantee against every attack and is not a substitute for server security headers. Please use device locking, current browser updates and safe backup handling. Do not share exported JSON backups publicly.

7. Children and geographic availability

MotoTrip is a general travel-planning tool and is not designed or marketed specifically for children. If targeting minors or collecting additional personal information later, applicable age and consent protections must be reassessed. This policy is written for the current web release with users including those in India.

8. Changes, questions and complaints

We may update this policy as the app changes, with a new effective date. For non-confidential support, use the public MotoTrip issue tracker; do not post personal data or trip histories there. For private support, privacy questions, data-related requests or complaints, contact Satish Kumar Gupta at satish9648291@gmail.com. Please avoid sending passwords, financial credentials or full trip-history backups by email unless strictly necessary.

Support & privacy contact: satish9648291@gmail.com (temporary contact address; may be updated in a future release). The public issue tracker should not be used to transmit personal data.
