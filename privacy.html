MotoTrip Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

This policy applies to MotoTrip – Ride Planner, a motorcycle and car trip planning and expense-sharing tool available at mototrip.github.io/Trip-Planner.

Information you enter

Depending on how you use MotoTrip, the app can store trip names, route start, multiple destinations, optional selected coordinates, dates and distances, estimated fuel and other travel costs, your bike name and specifications, gear checklists, rider names, expense entries, contributions, settlements, payment records and display preferences.

Where your data is stored

In this web-app version, those details are saved locally in your device/browser storage. MotoTrip does not provide an account, automatic cloud synchronization, or a server-side database for your trips. Data you enter is not automatically sent to the app developer. Other people using the same browser profile may be able to see data stored there.

Backups and deletion

You can export a JSON backup and restore it using the My trips section. Exported files are handled by your device and by any service where you choose to keep or share them; keep them private. To delete app-saved trip and expense data, open My trips → Delete all local data. You can also remove this site's data through your browser settings. Data remains on the device until you delete it or your browser clears site storage. Deleting locally stored data does not erase backup copies you previously exported.

Third-party services

When you choose Open Google Maps, MotoTrip passes the start point and destination/waypoint text you entered in a Google Maps URL. Google then processes that request under its own privacy policy. This website is hosted using GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process ordinary website requests or technical logs under its policies. When you open the interactive Route Map, map tiles load from OpenStreetMap. When you choose to search for a place or tap a map point, the app may send your search or selected coordinates to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim geocoding service to show location names. These requests also expose usual network information, such as an IP address, to those providers. The app does not automatically request device GPS location. Google Maps opens separately when you tap its directions links; its own privacy practices apply.

Advertising, analytics and payments

The provided web-app version contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, in-app purchase system, or credit-card payment collection. Its recorded settlements are manual bookkeeping entries, not actual money transfers. If features or third-party services are added later, this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration must be reviewed and updated.

Security and limitations

Your entries are stored in the browser's local storage, not encrypted by MotoTrip. Keep your device secure and protect exported backup files. The app's planning estimates may not reflect actual road conditions, fuel consumption or costs.

Children

MotoTrip is intended as a general travel planning utility, not specifically designed for children. It does not knowingly ask users to create accounts.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated when MotoTrip's functionality or data handling changes. The effective date above will be updated accordingly.

Contact

For privacy inquiries or app support, contact the MotoTrip developer through MotoTrip's GitHub issues page. Issues are public; do not post private trip information or personal details. You can also use the developer contact information shown on the app's Google Play listing when available.
