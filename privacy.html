Privacy Policy

Effective / last updated: 24 November 2026 · Owner & developer: Satish Kumar Gupta

This Privacy Policy describes the current MotoTrip – Ride Planner web/PWA release at mototrip.github.io/Trip-Planner and its optional Android Trusted Web Activity (TWA) preview, which opens the same website using an installed compatible browser. MotoTrip is operated by Satish Kumar Gupta, who is responsible for publishing this version. The policy covers local use, browser-managed TWA use and the optional Firebase-backed Shared Ride feature described below. Any future native SDKs or iOS release require a new privacy review.

1. Information entered in the app

You may enter trip names and routes (start, stops and destination), selected map coordinates, travel dates and distances, bike or car profile details, budgets, food/hotel/fuel/toll estimates, riding gear checklists, rider names, group expenses, payment/settlement records and theme preferences. Do not enter financial account credentials or passwords into trip, expense, or rider profile fields. Use passwords only in the dedicated Firebase login/sign-up form.

2. Local data storage and access

This web version primarily saves app entries in your browser's localStorage on your device. In offline mode there is no automatic synchronization. When the optional Shared Ride feature is configured and enabled, Firebase Authentication creates a device-linked anonymous account and Google Cloud Firestore stores the shared group name, rider names, expense entries, payment records, membership identifiers and update times. This information is shared among members who join that ride through an invitation link. Private trip planner and vehicle information remain local unless a user explicitly adds them to a shared expense record. Entered trip and expense records are not automatically uploaded to MotoTrip's owner. Shared Ride members can view and edit the group data and receive changes while online. Leaving a Shared Ride disconnects this device, but does not erase the remote shared group or revoke other participants. Anonymous Firebase IDs may change after browser storage is cleared, requiring rejoining through the invite link. Other users of the same device/browser profile may be able to access your records. The app does not encrypt your saved trip data itself.

2A. Android TWA preview and device storage

The Android TWA preview delegates web rendering, authentication cookies, camera and location permission prompts to a compatible installed browser. It does not use a separate app-owned WebView or a native analytics or advertising SDK in the current preview. Browser providers may apply their own service and privacy practices. The TWA preview stores website data in that browser's website-storage area; existing MotoTrip Android WebView data and login sessions are not automatically transferred. Keep the old app until you have verified your own JSON/Excel backup and restoration, and re-enter private emergency fields if needed. The Android preview is not yet the verified, signed Google Play release.

3. Map and hosting services

  • GitHub Pages: serves this website. Your browser connects to GitHub to load its files, which can involve IP addresses and technical request logs subject to GitHub's policies.
  • OpenStreetMap tile servers: loading the Route map requests map image tiles from an OpenStreetMap-operated service. Network metadata such as IP address is shared with that service.
  • Photon (Komoot): when you type at least three letters into a place-search field, your query is sent to Photon to display suggested locations, including district/state/country when available. When you explicitly tap My location and permit access, coordinates may be sent to Photon for reverse place-name lookup. Your IP address may be visible to this third-party map provider. The demo service has availability and rate limitations.
  • Google Maps: opening a directions link sends the entered route start, destination and limited waypoints to Google Maps. It opens separately and Google's policies then apply.

The app requests device GPS location only when you tap a My location button and grant browser permission. Coordinates from GPS or chosen search results may be stored in browser localStorage on your device and sent to Google Maps if you open driving directions. You can clear a location or delete all local data; your phone controls GPS permission. Exact GPS position can be sensitive: avoid sharing public backups containing route data.

4. Ads, analytics and payments

The current website has no app-owned analytics or advertising SDK, payment processor or money transfer system. Optional Shared Ride uses anonymous Firebase Authentication accounts. Expense settlements represent user-entered bookkeeping records, not executed bank transactions. New advertising, other cloud integrations or added Android/iOS SDKs would require review and a policy update.

5. Your choices, retention and deletion

Use My Trips → Export Backup to save a JSON backup. That file is unencrypted and is handled by your device and wherever you choose to store it. Use My Trips → Delete all local data to remove local trip and expense data; to remove rider profile and encrypted emergency information, use My Rider Profile → Delete local profile; you can also clear site storage in browser settings. Local records otherwise remain until deleted or cleared by the browser. Deletion on one device does not delete separately exported backups or third-party service logs. Local-data deletion does NOT delete Firebase-hosted Shared Ride records. Leaving the Shared Ride also does not erase remote data. To request deletion of cloud group records, contact the operator at the email below; ride members should avoid sharing unnecessary personal data.

6. Data security

The website is served over HTTPS. The app restricts browser scripts to its own origin and the Firebase SDK CDN through a Content Security Policy meta tag; this is not a guarantee against attacks and not a substitute for server-side security. Firebase Authentication and Firestore Security Rules restrict cloud group access, subject to correct deployment. Anyone you invite may view and modify the shared group. Invite links are access credentials: share them only with trusted people. Please use device locking, current browser updates and safe backup handling. Do not share exported backups or Shared Ride invitation links publicly.

7. Children and geographic availability

MotoTrip is a general travel-planning tool and is not designed or marketed specifically for children. If targeting minors or collecting additional personal information later, applicable age and consent protections must be reassessed. This policy is written for the current web release with users including those in India.

8. Changes, questions and complaints

We may update this policy as the app changes, with a new effective date. For non-confidential support, use the public MotoTrip issue tracker; do not post personal data or trip histories there. For private support, privacy questions, data-related requests or complaints, contact Satish Kumar Gupta at satish9648291@gmail.com. Please avoid sending passwords, financial credentials or full trip-history backups by email unless strictly necessary.

Support & privacy contact: satish9648291@gmail.com (temporary contact address; may be updated in a future release). The public issue tracker should not be used to transmit personal data.

Rider profiles, sign-in and emergency details (V5.7)

MotoTrip offers optional email/password Firebase Authentication and a no-account Guest mode. Firebase Authentication processes account email, authentication identifiers and login metadata according to its service practices. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are not saved by MotoTrip in browser localStorage. Email/password login only functions after the developer enables the Email/Password provider in Firebase Console.

Profile information may include a rider name, chosen vehicle type, optional gender, emergency contact name/telephone and self-reported blood group. The rider name and vehicle type are saved locally in readable form. Starting with V9.29, optional gender, emergency contact name/telephone and blood group are encrypted with AES-GCM before being saved in browser storage; a non-extractable device key is kept in browser IndexedDB, and older plaintext profile values are migrated on opening the app when secure storage is available. This is device-bound encryption, not end-to-end encryption and does not protect against malware or scripts running on the same website origin. If encrypted storage is unavailable, the app will not silently delete the older profile or save new emergency information in plaintext. Clearing site data may permanently erase the device key and encrypted information. V5.8 Group Safety can upload selected emergency information to Firestore only if you separately opt into sharing with a ride; location sharing has its own separate control. They are not end-to-end encrypted or securely hidden from someone with access to the unlocked device or browser profile. Emergency information is optional and is not medically verified.

Guest and registered accounts use separate local trip/Bill Split data spaces in this browser. The existing pre-update MotoTrip data remains in the Guest space; a new account can choose to copy it into its account-specific local space. Signing in on another device does not transfer trips, Bill Split history, or health/emergency profile details. Use the existing Excel/JSON backup and restore tools for transfers.

Under Rider Profile you can remove the locally saved emergency and other profile details, sign out, or request deletion of your Firebase email/password account. Account deletion may require recent authentication and does not automatically remove shared Firestore ride data, other members' records, or local backups. For remaining data-deletion requests, contact satish9648291@gmail.com.

Group Safety: optional emergency details and location (V5.8)

Shared Ride members can choose to share their rider name, self-reported blood group, emergency contact name and telephone with joined members. This is off by default and requires selecting Share my emergency info in Group Safety. Other private profile details including gender and account email are not shared by Group Safety. You can turn emergency sharing off again; cloud data removal requires an internet connection.

Update V9.27: Shared Ride live GPS/location sharing has been discontinued. The group no longer starts GPS watchers, uploads live coordinates or shows rider location links. The separate Route tab's optional My location lookup still works on your own device. For accounts that used the old group-location feature, MotoTrip attempts to clear previously shared coordinates from Firestore after that user reconnects; this cleanup needs internet and may not complete while an account remains inactive. Emergency contact sharing is still optional.

Safety cards are stored in the shared ride’s Firestore safety subcollection and may be read by currently authenticated members as controlled by the published Firestore Security Rules. Any person who joins through an invitation may become a member; share links only with trusted people. Each rider may remove their own safety card; cloud data, service logs or screenshots held by other riders may persist beyond that action. Members can call a voluntarily shared emergency contact using their phone’s dialer; no calls are made automatically.

Emergency-use limitation: Self-reported blood groups are not a substitute for clinical blood typing. GPS can be inaccurate or unavailable. This app is not an emergency dispatch, tracking guarantee or rescue service. In an emergency contact local emergency services directly. For deletion/support requests contact satish9648291@gmail.com.

QR Scanner and Camera Permission (V5.4)

When you tap Scan QR, MotoTrip requests camera access only to read a Shared Ride invitation code. QR camera frames are processed inside your browser and are not uploaded to our servers. The camera stops when scanning closes or you leave the page. Some browsers load the open-source jsQR decoder from jsDelivr, which receives a standard library-download request; the invitation image is not sent to that service. You can always join using an invite link instead.
