MotoTrip V4.5 — Fuel Expense Based Mileage Calculator
- Real Mileage Calculator mein Fuel filled (litres) ki jagah Total fuel expense (₹) hai.
- User distance travelled (km) aur total fuel expense (₹) enter karega.
- Calculator Planner ke Petrol ₹/L rate ko use karke km/L estimate karega.
- Example: 320 km, ₹950 fuel expense, ₹95/L => 32 km/L.
- Price badalne par result refresh hota hai; zero/invalid price ya invalid mileage par Apply disabled hai.
- 'Use mileage' phir 'Save Bike' press karne par bike mileage save hoga.
- Existing localStorage key 'mototrip-v1', old trips/expenses, route, privacy/legal docs and the original MotoTrip logo remain unchanged.
- Agar fuel ka rate alag alag raha ho to mileage ek estimate hai.
