WHAT CHANGED
- Mark paid now supports Undo / Unpaid in the Final Settlement area.
- After any settlement is marked paid, a Payment status section appears, displaying the newest payment plus a quick Undo / Unpaid button.
- Older payment entries are available under View older payments; the full Payment history also contains Undo / Unpaid.
- Undo does not delete the payment record; the record remains with Unpaid status so it can be marked paid again.
- Balances and Final Settlement automatically recalculate when status changes.
- Existing payment records without a status field are treated as Paid (backward compatibility).
- The local storage key and backup format are unchanged; exported JSON carries the paid/unpaid status.

USAGE
1. Expense Split > Final settlement > Mark paid.
2. Payment status > Undo / Unpaid if you tapped by mistake.
3. The amount becomes outstanding again, and the payment shows Unpaid.
4. For a genuine payment, use Mark paid again to reactivate that record.

TESTING/SAFETY
- Confirm before toggling payment status.
- Past trips, expenses, rider names, GPS route, and MotoTrip legal pages preserved.
- This is a PWA, not yet a Google Play Android App Bundle (.aab).
- Export JSON backup before replacing files on GitHub.
