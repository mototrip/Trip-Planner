MotoTrip V9.28 — Bill Split save bug fix

- Bill Split Save Trip / Update Trip now works without any Planner route/distance/fuel inputs.
- Group name can be changed directly in Bill Split when not in an active shared group.
- Bill-only trips are saved under Trips, with snapshot of members, expense history and transfers.
- Saved Bill Split can be reopened without entering Planner data.
- If a matching Planner trip exists, Bill Split updates its expenses but keeps route, distance and budget unchanged.
- No duplicate trip for same name. App localStorage/backup retained.
- PDF marks Bill-only reports correctly and hides unavailable Budget vs Actual comparison; expense history and settlements still included.
- CSV/Excel indicate Bill Split only and do not show fake km/estimated budgets.
- Old full-Planner Save Trip still requires complete Planner data.
