MotoTrip V7.6 — Collaborative Group Packing + Simple Zero Start

- Fresh users see zero distance and estimated cost until valid trip details are entered. Already-saved drafts and trips are preserved.
- Gear tab: while in a Shared Ride, all group members see one live packing matrix (Items | Me | other joined riders).
- Every rider may tick/untick ONLY their own column. Everyone can add/remove group custom items.
- Other riders see new packing ticks through Firestore live updates. Offline personal checklist is unchanged.
- Bill Split: redundant Record a payment accordion hidden; Mark paid and Payment history remain.
